# 🧠 X's Second Brain

Your home base. Everything connects back here.

> [!tip] How to capture an idea fast
> Just tell me an idea in chat and say *"add this to my second brain."* I'll write it into `01 - Ideas/` using the idea template, tag it, and link it back to this dashboard.

## 🗺️ Maps of Content

- [[Ideas MOC]] — every idea, captured and linked
- [[FlowState AI]] — your main project

## 📂 The PARA structure

| Folder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `01 - Ideas` | Raw thoughts, sparks, "what if" notes — the core of the brain |
| `02 - Projects` | Active things with a goal + deadline (e.g. FlowState AI) |
| `03 - Areas` | Ongoing responsibilities you maintain over time |
| `04 - Resources` | Reference material, research, things you're learning |
| `05 - Archive` | Done / inactive — out of the way but searchable |

## 🔎 Quick views

- Recent ideas: open `01 - Ideas` and sort by modified date
- Open the **Graph view** (left sidebar) to see how ideas connect

## ✅ Capture habit

The brain only gets valuable if ideas land here. Two ways:
1. **In the moment** — tell me an idea and ask me to save it.
2. **Batch** — paste a dump of notes and I'll split them into linked idea notes.
